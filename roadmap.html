Future phases

Build the assistant in layers.

Walker OS should grow from a reliable Pi-based voice hub into a multi-device assistant, not try to ship every ambitious feature before the foundation is stable.

Roadmap rule

Core, safety, and recovery come first. Hardware integrations, vehicle support, AR glasses, and autonomous actions should arrive only after the base system is testable and reversible.

Interactive roadmap

Major capability tracks.

These tabs keep the biggest ideas grouped so buyers and builders can understand what is real first and what comes later.

Phase 1

Voice that works hands-free.

Walker OS should listen through speech-to-text, respond through text-to-speech, support typed fallback input, and expose a privacy mode that keeps sensitive commands local.

  • Wake phrase and push-to-talk modes
  • Local transcript history with delete controls
  • Voice profiles for different speakers and environments

Vehicle direction

Cars should start as read-only context.

Walker may eventually connect to vehicles through OBD-II or companion apps, but the safe first path is monitoring, maintenance reminders, route context, and voice prompts. Steering, braking, throttle, or safety-critical control should stay out of scope unless there is a real compliance plan.

Good first step

Telemetry

Battery, maintenance, engine codes, trip context, location-aware reminders, and driver-safe summaries.

Useful interface

Voice prompts

Short spoken alerts, no distracting screens, manual mute, and clear disable controls.

Boundary

No vehicle control first

Keep Walker advisory until safety, liability, legal, and hardware requirements are fully understood.
